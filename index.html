Badminton

How to Play

To play badminton, start by serving to the other player or team on the opposite side of the court. Then, wait for them to hit the shuttlecock back to your side.

Equipment

Badminton equipment and facilities include rackets, shuttlecocks, courts, nets, and posts. Badminton rackets are made from lightweight and durable materials

Techniques

CLEAR, SMASH, DROP, DRIVE! - An overview of the different types of badminton strokes and when to use them.

History

Badminton has a rich history, originating in India and later developed in England. It became an Olympic sport in 1992. The roots of the sport can be traced to the old game battledore and shuttlecock, which was played in ancient Greece, China, and India.
